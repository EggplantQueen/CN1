# CN1 LAB — MUTANT laboratory development checkpoint
Date: November 3, 2026

This is a dated owner-reported concept and private-preview update for NEX,
not an independent check of the live website or game.

## MUTANT — development now centered on the laboratory

The creator is refining MUTANT as a game of **experiments, missions and
discoveries within a 3D laboratory**, rather than another outdoor
exploration game repeating Demon Drift. In the private laboratory work,
a 3D bioreactor/vat and a cybernetic eggplant seed are present, with
camera framing and presentation being refined. This is private preview
work, not proof of a public playable release.

The intended loop is to experiment inside the laboratory and discover
effects, anomalies, useful items or problems. The creator also wants
players to share noteworthy discoveries or problems back into the
Telegram community. Links to Demon Drift, guild cooperation and exchanging
or using discovered equipment are **design goals**, not launched functions
verified by these notes.

The related collective-creature **GENESIS** concept is still a planned
part of the CN1/MUTANT story. There is no running official 14-day
reveal, verified collective vote, paid XP system, NFT mint or released
guild-integrated creature on this checkpoint.
