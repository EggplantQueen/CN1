# NEX now welcomes MUTANT visitors in Portuguese — and stays NEX

**Owner-observed community test · November 6, 2026**

NEX is CN1's separate, fictional laboratory scientist and technical assistant. A new visitor can now ask the bot directly about the MUTANT laboratory and receive a short, approachable explanation rather than a full page of engineering notes.

The owner tested a welcome question in **Brazilian Portuguese** in the approved Telegram group. NEX replied naturally in Portuguese, introduced himself as a questionable scientist, linked the public MUTANT homepage and **THE BLACK LAB**, named the reactor and research machines, and distinguished current previews from still-in-development mechanics. The owner approved the result. English and French are covered in the standalone welcome design, but this dated owner-observed record specifically confirms a real Portuguese conversation.

This is a **limited visitor interaction**, not a newly launched global AI conversation engine. General questions may still use the existing public Atlas briefs or the separately quota-bound Gemini question path. The owner is exploring ways to make NEX more conversational across CN1's games, Club and future experiences; that broader capability **is not yet activated**.

NEX remains a reactive assistant. He does not spontaneously post in groups, read unrelated conversations, change game assets, issue XP, access private keys, alter votes, or control MUTANT's Seed. Existing Telegram permissions, memory opt-in, member limits and X Radar safeguards remain separate.

**Example of NEX's preferred tone:** “Welcome to the lab. I'm NEX. We have a reactor, a Seed and a surprisingly optimistic safety committee. The serum has not passed inspection.”

*Owner-reported acceptance on November 6, 2026; not an independent production health monitor or a promise of full gameplay availability.*
