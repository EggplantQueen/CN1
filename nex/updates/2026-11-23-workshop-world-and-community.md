# CN1 progress: the workshop, the world and the community

**Public project checkpoint · 23 November 2026 · owner reports and reviewed project records**

There has been substantial work since NEX's previous public archive update on 6 November. It includes physical assembly tests, new 3D models, electronics planning and the next shared space in CN1's world.

This note reviews the latest known state on 23 November. It does not imply that every milestone happened today. Dates below identify documented checkpoints where available. A working prototype, an approved design and an opened public feature are different kinds of progress.

## NEX-01: a physical desk companion

The physical NEX figurine and miniature desk laboratory are progressing through hands-on multicolour printing and early electronics work. Current model work focuses on cleaner colour separation, tongue and tooth details, assembly and fitting the replacement torso screen.

The architecture approved on **22 November** makes NEX-01 a Wi-Fi companion connected to our own NEX service. Sound, display, presence and lighting belong to the device; conversation is handled through the service. The current screen choice is a **Waveshare 1.83-inch display**, replacing the unavailable round screen. NEX remains fixed, with servos intended for the scanner and accessories.

The architecture decision is complete. Working voice firmware, final screen integration and a connection with the Telegram assistant still need implementation and tests. The desk figurine and the Telegram bot are separate projects.

## La Poigne du Neant: the dice tower has been assembled

The owner has confirmed that the tower is **assembled and fits its tray correctly**. That is a real physical assembly result.

At the latest owner checkpoint, the slide was still being printed. Remaining supports, the full dice path and decorative finishing were still being worked on. Sound, lighting and a possible impact-triggered effect belong to electronics preparation; this update does not claim those effects are already working.

## Original dice: two approved directions and a real D20 study

The two approved art directions are **Eclat comprime** and **Rosace repliee**: compressed mineral forms and folded rose-window architecture, designed to belong with the tower.

By the **20 November** project checkpoint, a sculptural D20 existed as real geometry with a render made from the model, thick faceted forms and recessed numbers. Its resting surfaces still require correction. This study is **not ready to print or play**, and its final artistic fidelity has not been approved.

Physical print tests, painting, roll behaviour, resin masters and moulds remain further stages. The earlier panelled D6 is not the current approved sculptural direction. No finished dice range or commercial release is announced.

## Workshop tools that help build the physical projects

**CN1 LAB LINK**, documented on **22 November**, is the first design for a modular electronics workbench. CAD and prototype files cover mechanically connected holders for breadboards, small boards, terminal blocks and cables, plus practical wire supports. Geometry has been checked digitally; fit with printed parts and real components still needs physical testing.

The painting grip has also received practical owner feedback. Its handle ergonomics, textures and base were approved. The adjustment wheel, little-finger support and support rods are still being refined for comfort and strength.

The **Eggplant Queen voxel trophy** concept has been approved: a sculpture and broad stable base intended as one multicolour print, with a separate replaceable nameplate. This remains an approved design, without a confirmed final production run.

## Tree of the Abyss and the future Guild Hall

On **23 November**, the Guild Hall reached a new architectural checkpoint. An isolated layout preview exists, and the owner approved a more monumental room centred on a **colossal Tree of the Abyss**.

The future Underworld elevator is intended to sit **inside the trunk and root base**, with a separate route to MUTANT's Black Lab. The goal is one shared meeting place, eventually reachable from the Club and a dedicated entry page. Visitors should not have to finish Demon Drift to enter it.

Detailed scenery, lighting, a few subtle animations and performance checks are staged work. The preview is not connected to the live Club. The new hall, market, Underworld travel and item economy have not opened through this checkpoint.

## Black Lab: the old startup blocker has been resolved

The later **6 November** owner checkpoint confirms that the Black Lab loads correctly in Edge and that the Seed's X-RAY interaction works. The previous note about remaining on the calibration screen is historical and **must not be repeated as the current state**.

The fitted purple egg, clearer reactor glass, subtle bubbles and surface cleanup were approved. X-RAY shows a spectral treatment of the egg's surface. A creature inside it and persistent collective evolution remain future work.

The local G03-G05 experiment loop produced a stable report, and a real Black Box Telegram share with the correct **/black-lab/** address was confirmed on **7 November**. Later owner feedback also accepted the industrial ceiling, coloured vapor, equipment placement, biotech pod location and rear-shelf dressing.

Machine controls and fluid animation continue to be developed in stages. These local training interactions are not proof of global Seed changes, guild inventory or shared rewards.

## Demon Drift and the wider tabletop direction

The **10 November** owner checkpoint records Chapter II's eight-direction movement update as installed, with movement, dash, combat, smoke, Seed, Warden, Webheart and cinematic checks reported successful. Chapter I had already been reported publicly released. This does not certify the current public accessibility of every chapter.

The unfinished Chapter III remains on hold. A documented transition concept connects its future story to the same Abyss Tree, Guild Hall and MUTANT setting; that transition is not a newly released chapter.

The wider creative work aims to connect **miniatures, dice and towers with CN1's existing universe**, building on the Club and Black Lab. A complete tabletop rules system, campaign and physical-to-game rewards remain to be designed and validated.

## Community: an image test and continuing stories

On **22 November**, the owner tested image recognition by **CN1 in Telegram** and explicitly reported success. This is a concrete test result. It is not proof that every image is supported, that all conversations are monitored or that the separate **@CN1Nex_bot** has the same capability.

The recurring Sunday comics continue as CN1 storytelling. Recent requested themes include Demon Drift and learning 3D printing while starting the NEX figurine's electronics. These are creative directions, without an invented episode number or an assertion that a specific new episode has been published.

NEX's community voice should use clear English or Brazilian Portuguese. Its project archive now includes this physical and creative work alongside the games and laboratory.

## How NEX should describe this checkpoint

When asked for news, recent progress, what's new, quoi de neuf or novidades, start with the latest dated overview and relevant project records. Concrete prototype work and approved design milestones are meaningful updates even before a public launch.

Do not turn an absence of a new game release into “nothing new.” If the archive is old or synchronization has failed, state the date of the latest available information. Do not imply access to unrecorded work.

A short example:

> There has been plenty happening in the workshop. The dice tower is assembled and fits its tray, NEX-01 is progressing through print and electronics work, and two original dice directions have been approved. The future Guild Hall is taking shape around a colossal tree. Some of this is still prototyping, but it is real progress. The lab has apparently expanded into the furniture department. 🍆
