# CN1 MUTANT — LAB WOW: the cardboard consoles are losing their jobs
**Public project update · November 4, 2026 · owner-reported and source cross-checked**

MUTANT is becoming a **walkable experimental 3D laboratory**, not a second Demon Drift and not yet a finished game. The current construction pass replaces provisional box-shaped control stations with **real original 3D (GLB/Tripo) machines**, integrated one by one. The work is intentionally modular: one machine can be improved without rebuilding the room or breaking established interactions.

## What has visibly advanced

The LAB WOW layout is much more spacious than its earlier prototype (the designed room is **26 × 22 × 7.2 scene units**), with practical routes between machines. The enlarged containment **Reactor + cybernetic Seed** is the centrepiece. **THE RELAY** scanner remains accessible from both sides, while primarily front-operated equipment is placed along the walls.

The owner's construction sequence has incorporated real 3D hardware for **THE DISTILLER XXL**, **THE CHILLER** coolant tower, **THE BLACK BOX**, **PRESSURE REGULATOR**, and the right-side **POWER CONTROL PANEL**. The power panel **R9F** has a dated owner-provided installation/HTTPS receipt and explicit visual approval on November 4; this is a confirmed checkpoint, not an assumption from a GitHub commit.

The **CONTAINMENT LOCK** (the G04 sealing station) was subsequently **visually approved by the owner**. Its original GLB and independent render module are archived, but this public note does not assert an independent server receipt for the R9G version. Do not silently promote approval to a certified live deployment.

The replacement **industrial ceiling light** (R10A) was **prepared and tested offline**, using an original multipart GLB above the containment reactor, but no successful installation/visual-acceptance receipt was confirmed in the reviewed evidence. The owner's subsequent **R10B direction** is a larger, more centred fixture, a less crowded ceiling, and only a handful of neon elements with subtle flicker and light falling onto the floor. It is a design refinement, **not a shipped release**.

## The work we have *not* finished

- **Physical animation and feedback:** give real functions to visible mechanical buttons, switches, handwheels, levers and locking parts, with understandable movement and feedback when players interact.
- **Fluids and machinery:** make laboratory liquids and coolant visually convincing (flow, circulation, reactions and containment effects). A still texture or coloured cylinder is not a finished fluid simulation.
- **Atmosphere and decoration:** polish the reactor glass, pipes, light spill, controlled flicker, smoke/steam, warning cues, wall detailing and dramatic industrial ambience without filling walking paths with obstacles.
- **Gameplay integration:** keep iterating on G03 (vial preparation and scan), G04 (pressure, cooling, power, sealing and containment) and G05 (BLACK BOX consequences); connect the **physical machinery** to those interactions instead of merely leaving static 3D props. A polished mission flow remains the goal.
- **Validation:** browser walkthrough, mobile performance, reachability and checking that new components do not break the approved machines. Planned shared guild items, collectible inventories or later Demon Drift connections are not yet demonstrated as live.

The existing G03/G04/G05 **local-practice mechanics** and their logic should not be confused with a fully animated laboratory or a global persistent creature. **GENESIS has not hatched; no global Seed evolution, public voting, NFT ownership or guild economy is being claimed here.**
