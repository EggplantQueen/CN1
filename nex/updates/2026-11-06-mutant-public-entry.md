## Development progress versus finished gameplay

The expanding laboratory is built around the 3D reactor and Seed and independent machine components, including THE RELAY, DISTILLER, CHILLER, BLACK BOX, pressure/power stations and containment hardware. Gameplay experiments around the vial, scanning and containment exist in local practice. The owner has approved progress in the actual 3D art, room layout and equipment, but **fully animated mechanical controls, convincing flowing liquids and end-to-end polish are still development work**.

The branded Black Lab URL responds, but **a complete 3D start and playthrough on that alias have not been verified by the latest receipt**. A prior browser test showed the startup screen waiting during system calibration. Do not claim the gameplay has been fully fixed merely because the public page title and links work.

This is **not** evidence of live global Seed changes, collectible item transfers, real member inventory, Telegram voting, Guild rewards, or NFT gameplay.
