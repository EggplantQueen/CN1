# CN1 workshop and world progress — 25 November 2026

This dated editorial record summarizes owner reports and project evidence reviewed on 25 November. It distinguishes installed work, prepared files and physical tests that are still pending.

## Guild Hall: the Club entrance is installed

The Guild Hall has progressed beyond the isolated layout preview recorded on 23 November. Its large circular entrance in the Club is owner-approved, and the installed entrance files were checked online on 25 November. The accepted colossal-tree hall is the setting for the project's future social and expedition direction.

The next prepared update adds the corresponding portal on the hall side, a deliberate return route to the Club and a richer animated mist interior. That update has not yet been confirmed installed or given its final browser playtest. The portal work does not establish a new marketplace, Underworld destination, shared-item system or reward economy.

## Dice tower: a real prototype drives a new exit design

La Poigne du Neant has a real assembled prototype. Subsequent owner tests on 23 November found mechanical details that still needed correction: nut accommodation, a protruding screw head and a final exit that slowed the dice too much.

The latest fabrication design extends the existing slide into a rounded chute, turning gently left, with accessible nut pockets. The new model and its numerical geometry and assembly checks are prepared. Printing and real dice-roll tests of this exit are still required. Sound, lighting and the complete finished assembly are not yet presented as validated.

## Tabletop terrain: a tested connector and four sculpted tile files

The Sanctuaire des Aubryths terrain now has four distinct sculpted floor-tile files. Each tile is 160 mm square; the first four compose a 320 mm square sector around the existing Heart and reservoir scenery. The visual thread is a laboratory gradually overtaken by roots, while the larger scenery remains removable.

The owner explicitly approved the fit of the small connector that is fitted and removed from above. That connector is reused unchanged in the new tiles. Individual 3MF and STL files, layout checks and geometry checks are prepared.

This physical connector test does not establish the flatness or final assembly fit of full-size tiles. The large tiles, new edge caps and actual play arrangement still need their own physical trials. The terrain is part of developing a coherent CN1 tabletop setting; no finished rules, balanced campaign or public product launch is announced.

## Continuing NEX-01 work

NEX-01 remains a physical desk prototype with an approved Wi-Fi companion architecture. Finished voice firmware and screen integration still need to be demonstrated.

These records describe what has actually been approved, prepared or installed and keep the remaining tests explicit.
