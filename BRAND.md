# CN1 — Public Visual Guide

CN1 should look like **CN1** everywhere.

Not a random anime girl with an eggplant pasted on top.

## The character

Public-facing CN1 visuals should preserve the recognizable core:

- adult stylized woman;
- pale complexion;
- long messy warm-blonde hair;
- half-lidded pink eyes;
- narrow sleepy / deadpan face;
- pink + black master identity;
- black studded choker with centered pink heart;
- eggplant motif used with restraint;
- dry, unimpressed, internet-native attitude.

The exact internal character canon is maintained privately. This page is only the public-facing summary.

## Default mood

Think:

**bored gamer queen who somehow has infrastructure now.**

Good:
- slouched;
- deadpan;
- casual;
- sarcastic;
- gaming;
- building;
- hanging around the CLUB;
- staring at something that probably broke;
- mildly disappointed in the internet.

Avoid:
- sexy posing as the default;
- vulgar or suggestive framing;
- cleavage/body-part emphasis;
- childlike proportions;
- generic e-girl drift;
- blue drool;
- turning CN1 into a corporate mascot.

## Visual world

The wider CN1 world leans toward:

- dark environments;
- pink neon;
- black;
- eggplant-purple accents;
- old-internet / degen energy;
- games;
- bots;
- chat/community;
- club/music spaces;
- controlled clutter;
- dry jokes hidden in the environment.

It should feel built, lived-in and slightly questionable.

Not polished startup stock art.

## Public asset rule

Public CN1 artwork and branding are **not automatically licensed for reuse** just because they are visible in this repository.

If an asset is explicitly released for reuse later, its terms will be stated next to that asset.
